## 3. Who Uses It

The platform has five user roles:

1. **Candidate:** Searches opportunities, checks eligibility and applies through the correct application route.
2. **Content Editor:** Adds, updates and verifies opportunity listings.
3. **Referral Operations Admin:** Manages referral requests and tracks referral-related activities.
4. **Finance Admin:** Handles financial operations and related records.
5. **Super Admin:** Manages the overall platform, users, permissions and administration.

**Pilot Note:** During the pilot phase, you will perform all administrative roles.

## 4. What the Pilot Includes

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will include:

- Public opportunity listings and search.
- Candidate registration and accounts.
- Consent management.
- Referral tracking.
- Administrative tools.
- Basic reports and analytics.

## 5. What It Does Not Do

The pilot version will not include:

- Scraping opportunities from LinkedIn.
- Guaranteeing jobs, interviews or referrals.
- Storing passwords for external websites.
- Automatically submitting applications to every platform.
- Guaranteeing that every listed opportunity will remain available.

## 6. How We Measure Success

We will measure the success of the pilot using three key metrics:

| Metric | What We Measure |
|---|---|
| Candidate Registrations | Number of invited candidates who successfully register. |
| Completed Profiles | Number of registered candidates who complete their profiles. |
| Verified Opportunities | Number of opportunity listings verified within the last 14 days. |

### Pilot Success Criteria

The pilot will be evaluated based on candidate registrations, profile completion rates and the freshness of verified opportunity listings.
